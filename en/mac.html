A Belgian Apple keyboard

A Belgian Apple keyboard has different characters printed on it than a PC keyboard, for example @ left of the 1. With the PC layout, many keys then type something else. If you use such a keyboard, type this after installing:

sudo APPLE=yes be-azerty-fix system

Are the key left of the 1 and the key next to the left Shift swapped? Then also type:

sudo be-azerty-fix apple-iso-keys on

Good to know

  • Option works as AltGr. Caps Lock works as on Linux.
  • The setting applies to every keyboard on the computer, including on the login screen. That is why it refuses when no Apple keyboard is plugged in.
  • The text console and the disk unlock screen keep the PC layout.
  • Not yet tested on a real Apple keyboard. In a test container, 333 of the 367 macOS characters matched. The difference is Caps Lock, which works differently on a Mac.

Back to a PC keyboard: sudo APPLE=no be-azerty-fix system.
