Your Belgian AZERTY, as on Windows.

On Linux Mint and Zorin OS, the Belgian layout has been correct for a long time. What is missing is the behaviour you are used to from Windows. We add it with two small packages and the keyd program:

  • Ctrl+Alt works as AltGr. Ctrl+Alt+é gives @, and Ctrl+Alt+E gives €.
  • Caps Lock as on Windows. With Caps Lock on, the top row gives the digits. On Mint this is on by default; on Zorin you switch it on yourself.
  • You choose what the numeric keypad key types: a period or a comma.
  • The layout stays Belgian. Also for new users and after a settings reset. Your other layouts stay in place.

What we don't do

  • No Linux distribution of our own.
  • No ISO files to download.
  • No cookies, no tracking.

What about the rest of the world?

Belgian first, PC and Mac, all the way. Other countries follow later, one country at a time.

The goal

We want the Linux projects to adopt this fix themselves. Until then, this is an unofficial community project.
