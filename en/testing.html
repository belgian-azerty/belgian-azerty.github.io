What is tested, and what is not

Tested

  • Linux Mint 22.3 and Zorin OS 18.1, in emulators and on one laptop (a Lenovo ThinkPad T14s).
  • The changes in 0.9.10, 0.9.11 and 0.9.12 themselves: only in test containers. 0.9.12 types the same as 0.9.11.
  • Mint 0.9.9 as a USB stick on the real laptop (reported by Younes).
  • Windows and Linux compared: with both accent options, 306 of the 308 Belgian Windows characters match. The last two are AltGr+4 and AltGr+5, and keyd handles those.
  • Ubuntu 24.04 and Debian 13 in test containers, including typing on a GNOME desktop without a screen: the same result as on Mint and Zorin.
  • A full package upgrade from Ubuntu 24.04 to 26.04 and from Debian 13 to the next Debian, in test containers.
  • Fedora, openSUSE, Arch, Void, NixOS, Gentoo, Slackware and Solus: in test containers, without a desktop. Five Linux families ›
  • Fedora 45 on a desktop in an emulator: typing works as on Mint, but SELinux blocks the system keyboard setting. Fix to follow.

Not yet tested

  • A real Apple keyboard.
  • 0.9.10 to 0.9.12, and Zorin 0.9.9, on real hardware.
  • Ubuntu's and Mint's own upgrade tools.
  • Systems other than Mint and Zorin on a real desktop or a real PC.
  • A second USB keyboard.

Status with the Linux projects: nothing accepted yet. Which part belongs to which project is in MAINTAINERS.md.
